# Hallmark adaptation notice

Joyally Design uses Hallmark as design-method research under the MIT License.

Adapted concepts: Floor, Reflex and Finish review tiers; a curated subset of default-pattern checks; and recording a before-state so redesigns preserve existing capability.

Joyally modifications: conditional product-aware rules, explainable DOM waivers and answers, real-browser computed-style checks, and a DOM contract covering links, forms, fields, IDs, test hooks, headings and media. No Hallmark theme catalog, generated page, component or visual asset is included.
