# Provenance

This skill is a general-purpose extraction from a production writing workflow. Its natural-language quality checks consolidate practical experience and ideas from these sources:

- `skill-deslop` by Stephen D. Turner, MIT;
- `tropes.fyi` by ossama.is;
- `stop-slop` by Hardik Pandya, MIT;
- `deslop-text` by Adam Dunkels, whose license was not specified in the source notes.

The rules in this repository were rewritten and reorganized for Chinese and English articles, proposals, reports, product copy, and documentation. Project-specific craft rules, publication gates, private paths, business metrics, and workflows are intentionally excluded.

The logic reference uses standard distinctions from deductive, inductive, abductive, analogical, causal, and informal reasoning. It is an original operational synthesis for writing workflows rather than a reproduction of one textbook or argumentation framework.

The 紫升风格 profile was distilled from the author's public posts on `blog.zisheng.pro` using corpus-level patterns and representative articles. Recent 2026 posts anchor the current reasoning style; the 2021 component-library breakout article anchors long-form tutorial personality and reader engagement. The profile records writing decisions and anti-mimicry boundaries; it does not copy source passages into generated work.

The book-writing workflow is an original synthesis informed by publicly available Agent Skills and writing systems. [`wgwtest/novel-writing`](https://github.com/wgwtest/novel-writing) (MIT) contributed the idea of near-field/far-field context and concrete continuity review; [`JeroTan/novel-writer-english`](https://github.com/JeroTan/novel-writer-english) (MIT) demonstrated a staged constitution/specification/plan/write/edit/review lifecycle; [`danjdewhurst/story-skills`](https://github.com/danjdewhurst/story-skills) (MIT) demonstrated Markdown-first story bibles and deterministic continuity state; [`luquiluke/claude-book-skill`](https://github.com/luquiluke/claude-book-skill) (MIT) demonstrated a nonfiction project split among book contract, voice profile, research, manuscript, versions, and output. [`zy-zmc/tianming-skill`](https://github.com/zy-zmc/tianming-skill) was reviewed for modular routing and state-writeback ideas; no text or project-specific terminology was copied.

The copyright gate was written from first principles and checked against the 2020 [`中华人民共和国著作权法`](https://www.ncac.gov.cn/xxfb/flfg/flfg_532/202103/t20210309_50530.html) published by the National Copyright Administration of China, especially the exclusive rights in Article 10, derivative-work rules in Articles 13 and 16, and quotation limits in Article 24. It also uses the U.S. Copyright Office's [writer guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/engage/writers/), [copyright basics](https://www.copyright.gov/what-is-copyright/), and [Fair Use Index](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/fair-index.html) for the distinction between ideas/facts and protected expression and for conservative exception review. The gate intentionally requires evidence and human review beyond what those sources legally mandate; it is an operational risk control, not a restatement of any one jurisdiction's law.

The AI technical-book reference is an original operational synthesis of public author guidance, technical-writing standards, executable-book practice, and AI reproducibility frameworks. Reader qualification and instructional scaffolding were informed by Manning's [author resources](https://livebook.manning.com/book/author-resources/chapter-2); proposal and editorial-quality concerns by [No Starch Press](https://nostarch.com/writeforus); code, terminology, and production details by the [O'Reilly Style Guide](https://oreillymedia.github.io/production-resources/styleguide/), [Microsoft code-example guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/developer-content/code-examples), and [Google Technical Writing](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing). The tutorial/how-to/explanation/reference distinction was informed by [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/).

Executable-book patterns were checked against [Dive into Deep Learning](https://d2l.ai/), the [Hugging Face course](https://huggingface.co/docs/course/chapter1/1), and [Jupyter Book execution](https://jupyterbook.org/stable/execution/). AI evidence controls were informed by the [NeurIPS Paper Checklist](https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist), [Model Cards](https://research.google/pubs/model-cards-for-model-reporting/), [NIST AI TEVV](https://www.nist.gov/ai-test-evaluation-validation-and-verification-tevv), and ACM artifact-evaluation practice. Documentation versioning was checked against [GitHub Docs](https://docs.github.com/en/contributing/writing-for-github-docs/versioning-documentation). These sources contributed principles and verification targets, not reusable prose or a copied chapter structure.

The [`fastai/fastbook`](https://github.com/fastai/fastbook) repository was inspected only as a high-level example of notebook-based teaching and end-to-end progression. Its prose explicitly restricts redistribution and modification, so no prose, examples, exercises, distinctive sequence, or other protected expression was copied into this skill. The same non-copying rule applies to every commercial author guide and technical book reviewed during research, regardless of whether its web page is publicly readable.

The open-source project reference gate was checked against GitHub's [repository licensing guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository), [Terms of Service](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service), and [commit-permalink guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/working-with-files/using-files/getting-permanent-links-to-files): ordinary project citation and independently expressed ideas are separated from copying protected repository material. When expressive material is reused, a public repository without an applicable license remains under default copyright, and mutable branch links are not stable source evidence. License identification and compound expressions use the [SPDX License List](https://spdx.org/licenses/). Expressive-use clearance remains path-specific because a project can contain multiple licenses and separately governed materials.

License-family routing was checked against the [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd), the [GNU license FAQ](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.html.en), the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.html), and [Creative Commons software guidance](https://creativecommons.org/faq/). The reference restates operational checks in original language; it does not reproduce license text or offer a legal conclusion about any particular repository. Trademark separation follows the [Open Source Initiative FAQ](https://opensource.org/faq), which distinguishes software permissions from rights in names and marks.

The similarity gate is an original deterministic implementation of long-paragraph normalization, exact matching, SimHash candidate generation, character-shingle Jaccard comparison, and sequence similarity. Interactive unknown-source discovery uses the web search capability available to the active Codex session and records review evidence; the standalone checker cannot call that conversation tool. Its optional automation-provider boundary was checked against the official [Brave Search API](https://api-dashboard.search.brave.com/api-reference/web/search/get). Commercial-provider routing was checked against the official [Copyleaks file-submission API](https://docs.copyleaks.com/reference/actions/authenticity/submit-file/), [Turnitin Core API](https://developers.turnitin.com/turnitin-core-api/migrating-from-ithenticate-api), [万方文献相似性检测服务](https://check.wanfangdata.com.cn/), [万方科研诚信个人服务](https://jc.wanfangdata.com.cn/), [万方报告真伪查询](https://truth.wanfangdata.com.cn/), and the [万方数据云平台](https://cloud.wanfangdata.com.cn/) description of its contract-oriented API gateway. These services informed integration, evidence, and privacy boundaries only; no proprietary algorithm, report text, private endpoint, or vendor implementation was copied. The gate deliberately treats all similarity output as review evidence rather than an infringement or plagiarism verdict.

The publishing-requirements gate is an original synthesis of professional editorial stages, publisher-specific style control, deterministic manuscript preflight, and named human review evidence. The mainland-China baseline was checked against the National Press and Publication Administration's [《图书质量管理规定》](https://www.nppa.gov.cn/xxgk/fdzdgknr/zcfg_210/bmgz_213/202112/t20211208_442149.html) and release notice for [CY/T 266—2023](https://www.nppa.gov.cn/xxfb/tzgs/202306/t20230630_718503.html), plus the official national-standard records for [GB/T 7714—2025](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=C6CE52E55AC09B9C79A20AEA77CEDD14), [GB/T 15834—2011](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=22EA6D162E4110E752259661E1A0D0A8), [GB/T 15835—2011](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=F5DAC3377DA99C8D78AE66735B6359C7), and [GB/T 7713.2—2022](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=0B963916637B8F34B295FCF4A51A1BE5). Citation-metadata and update-status checks were verified against [Crossref metadata retrieval](https://www.crossref.org/services/metadata-retrieval/) and [Crossmark](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/crossmark/). The Skill records source-of-truth routing and verification targets; it does not reproduce protected standard text or claim that an automated scan proves regulatory compliance.

Public Agent Skills were reviewed for workflow ideas before this gate was implemented. [`LVTD-LLC/skills` self-publishing production](https://github.com/LVTD-LLC/skills/tree/main/skills/self-publishing-production) separates developmental readiness, copyediting, proofreading, layout, and final proof; [`lensetek/Book-Author-Agent-Skills`](https://github.com/lensetek/Book-Author-Agent-Skills) separates citation integrity, manuscript structure, layout, DOCX export, and human review; [`obra/the-elements-of-style`](https://github.com/obra/the-elements-of-style) emphasizes specific, concrete, concise prose. Their mechanisms informed review decomposition only. No publisher preset, project-specific workflow, source text, or code was copied, and low-adoption or unsupported defaults were not treated as authoritative publishing rules.
